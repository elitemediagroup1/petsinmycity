Our Story

Pet care had become overwhelming. We set out to fix that.

If you have ever tried to find a trustworthy answer for your pet, you already know the problem. There are countless websites, endless opinions, and advice that constantly contradicts itself. One article says one thing, the next says the opposite, and somewhere in the middle is a worried pet owner just trying to do right by their dog or cat.

Local resources were even harder. Finding a vet, an emergency clinic, a groomer, or a boarding option in your own city often meant digging through scattered directories that had no real local knowledge and no sense of what actually matters in a moment of stress.

PetsInMyCity was created to bring everything together — clear guidance, helpful tools, local resources, and honest recommendations — in one calm, dependable place. We are part of Elite Media Group LLC, and we built this resource because pet owners deserve better than confusion.

Meet Lucy

Say hello to Lucy, your AI pet assistant

Lucy is the friendly heart of PetsInMyCity — and she’s far more than a chatbot. Think of her as a trusted guide, a local resource assistant, and an educational companion rolled into one: a tool built to help pet owners make informed decisions, not rushed ones. Lucy is designed to educate first. She listens, asks thoughtful questions to understand your situation, and recommends resources only when they’re genuinely appropriate — pointing you toward the right next step rather than the nearest sale.

  • ✨She’s AI-powered and always patient, day or night.
  • ❓She asks questions to truly understand what your pet needs.
  • 📍She helps you locate nearby vets, groomers, and emergency care.
  • 🦺She recommends trusted resources, not whatever pays the most.

Important: Lucy is here to educate and guide — she is not a replacement for a veterinarian. For medical concerns or emergencies, always contact a licensed vet right away.

Frequently Asked

About PetsInMyCity

What is PetsInMyCity?

PetsInMyCity is a free, pet-owner-first resource that brings together local pet services, emergency planning, expert-reviewed care guides, helpful tools, and honest product recommendations — guided by Lucy, our AI pet assistant. It is operated by Elite Media Group LLC.

Is PetsInMyCity free to use?

Yes. PetsInMyCity is always free for pet owners. We keep the site free through referral fees from affiliate partners when you choose to use their services — you never pay us directly.

Who is Lucy?

Lucy is our AI pet assistant. She is designed to educate first, ask questions to understand your situation, recommend trusted resources, and help you find nearby services. Lucy is not a replacement for a veterinarian, and medical emergencies always require a licensed vet.

How does PetsInMyCity recommend products?

We recommend products based on usefulness first — because they fit a real need, not because they pay the highest commission. Affiliate links help keep PetsInMyCity free, but they never determine what we suggest.

Can PetsInMyCity handle a pet emergency?

We can help you prepare and act quickly — for example, with our Pet Emergency Planner and Emergency Finder — but we are not a substitute for professional care. In any medical emergency, contact a licensed veterinarian or emergency clinic immediately.

Who builds and operates PetsInMyCity?

PetsInMyCity is built and operated by Elite Media Group, a company that creates technology and AI-powered platforms designed to help people make smarter everyday decisions. PetsInMyCity is the expression of that mission focused entirely on pet owners.

Why should I trust PetsInMyCity?

PetsInMyCity puts education before products, uses AI designed to help rather than persuade, connects you to real local resources, is transparent about affiliate relationships, follows a safety-first philosophy, and is built for pet owners rather than advertisers.
