# Austin — Research Checklist

> Companion to `DOSSIER.md`. The step-by-step investigation for Austin, per `../../docs/editorial/RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md`. Check items as reporting is completed; log every claim in `VERIFICATION_TRACKER.md`.

---

## Step 1 — Authoritative public data
- [ ] Census/ACS: Austin population, households, pet-relevant demographics
- [ ] NOAA climate normals: monthly temperature/heat profile, precipitation
- [ ] NWS Austin/San Antonio: heat advisories, flash-flood patterns & season
- [ ] EPA / state: air-quality patterns relevant to walking

## Step 2 — Local government & agencies
- [ ] Austin Parks & Recreation: park list, off-leash rules/areas, hours, closures
- [ ] City of Austin Code: leash ordinance, licensing, breed regulations
- [ ] Austin Animal Center: lost/found process, stray-hold, adoption, jurisdiction
- [ ] Travis County (and Williamson/Hays): county-level animal rules
- [ ] Watershed/flood authority: creek/greenbelt flood risk & alerts
- [ ] Transportation/parking: where parking is hard near key parks

## Step 3 — Local publications (current reality)
- [ ] Austin Monitor, Austin American-Statesman, KUT, KXAN: recent pet/park/heat/wildlife coverage

## Step 4 — Domain authorities
- [ ] Texas Parks & Wildlife: venomous snake species/ranges/season
- [ ] Vector/heartworm prevalence data (CDC/university/state)
- [ ] Texas Board of Veterinary Medical Examiners: licensed practices
- [ ] Austin-area veterinary society (if applicable)

## Step 5 — Community discussions (LEADS ONLY — verify everything)
- [ ] r/Austin, local dog Facebook/Nextdoor groups: harvest leads (which park is muddy, which vet is loved, coyote sightings) — mark each as a lead to verify, never as fact

## Step 6 — Expert voices
- [ ] Identify & contact experts per `EXPERT_SOURCES.md`

## Step 7 — Verify every meaningful claim
- [ ] Each claim entered in `VERIFICATION_TRACKER.md` with status/source/date

## Step 8 — Identify the central difference
- [ ] Articulate what genuinely distinguishes Austin pet life (must be supported by verified findings)

## Step 9 — Gate check (before writing)
- [ ] Publish gate satisfied (`../../docs/editorial/PUBLISH_GATE.md`)
- [ ] Local Authenticity Test passed (name-swap test especially)
- [ ] `MISSING_INFORMATION.md` cleared of blockers
- [ ] Review chain ready (`../../docs/editorial/EDITOR_CHECKLIST.md`)

## Priority "must-answer" questions for Austin
1. Real off-leash rules & conditions for the greenbelt and top parks.
2. Summer safe-walking windows & paw-burn/heat-illness guidance (safety floor).
3. Flash-flood season & which trails are affected (safety floor).
4. Venomous snake season & where (safety floor).
5. Emergency/24-hr vet coverage and gaps across the metro.
6. Leash/licensing ordinance surprises for newcomers.
7. Austin Animal Center lost-pet reclaim process (reunion-critical).
