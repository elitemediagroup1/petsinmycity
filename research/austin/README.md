# Austin — Gold-Standard Research Workspace

> **Status:** Research workspace. **NOT for publication.** Austin is being *prepared for investigation*, not rewritten.
> **Purpose:** Serve as the reference example of how a city is researched to newsroom depth before a single public word is changed.
> **Governed by:** `../../docs/editorial/EDITORIAL_OS.md` and companions. Uses the CITY dossier template (`../../docs/editorial/dossiers/TEMPLATE_CITY.md`).

---

## Why Austin

Austin already has a thin, affiliate-skewed public page (`/cities/austin/`) that we intend to *beat* — making it the ideal proving ground for the Editorial Operating System. When the Austin dossier is complete and the rewritten page passes the publish gate and Local Authenticity Test, it becomes the template every other city follows.

## Critical rule for this workspace

**Nothing in these files is a verified fact until its row in `VERIFICATION_TRACKER.md` says so.** The dossier here is a *research container*: framing questions, leads to chase, and open questions. Facts move from "to verify" to "verified" only after live confirmation against authoritative sources (per `../../docs/editorial/RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md`). This workspace deliberately contains **no invented Austin specifics.**

## Files in this workspace

- `DOSSIER.md` — the Austin research dossier (CITY template, framing only; to be filled by reporting).
- `KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH_SKELETON.md` — the Austin entities & relationships to populate as facts are verified.
- `RESEARCH_CHECKLIST.md` — the step-by-step investigation checklist for Austin.
- `VERIFICATION_TRACKER.md` — the live status of every meaningful claim (the source of truth).
- `MISSING_INFORMATION.md` — known gaps and open questions blocking publication.
- `EXPERT_SOURCES.md` — potential expert & interview opportunities (to be contacted).

## Definition of done (for Austin to move from research to writing)

1. Every meaningful claim in `VERIFICATION_TRACKER.md` is at least *Multi-source verified* / *Observed pattern*; safety-floor claims are *Verified/Officially confirmed* to Tier 1-2.
2. No item remains in `MISSING_INFORMATION.md` that blocks a reader question.
3. The draft passes the Local Authenticity Test (`../../docs/editorial/LOCAL_JOURNALISM.md`, Section 8) — including the name-swap test.
4. The full review chain (`../../docs/editorial/EDITOR_CHECKLIST.md`) is cleared with named reviewers.

Only then does writing begin, and only then is the public Austin page touched.
