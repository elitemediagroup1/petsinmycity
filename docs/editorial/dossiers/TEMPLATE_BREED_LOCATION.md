# Research Dossier — BREED × LOCATION Template

> Copy to `research/{city}/breeds/{breed}/DOSSIER.md`. Follows `README.md`. Statuses per `../PUBLISH_GATE.md`; tiers per `../RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md`.
>
> **Why this template matters most:** `Breed × Location` intersections are the platform's doorway-proof, high-value content (`../KNOWLEDGE_OS.md`, Section 3). A Great Dane in Austin genuinely differs from a Great Dane in Denver — altitude, heat, housing, laws, parks. The uniqueness must come from the *join*, verified, not from invented color. If swapping either the breed OR the city leaves the article mostly true, it fails.
