# Editor Checklist (Review & Approval)

> **Part of:** The PetsInMyCity Editorial Operating System (`EDITORIAL_OS.md`, Section 11).
> **Purpose:** The reviewable checklist that enforces the publish gate. No page publishes by its creator alone.
> **How to use:** Copy this checklist into the page's dossier (`dossiers/README.md`) or PR. Every box must be checked, with the responsible reviewer named, before final approval. Review depth scales with stakes (a routine update needs a lighter path; a new location or any safety content needs the full chain).

---

## Stage 1 — Research review
- [ ] The dossier exists and is complete (sources, verification log, findings, open questions).
- [ ] Reporting went deep enough that the page's central "what makes this place different" is clear.
- [ ] No meaningful claim is still **Needs further reporting**.
- Reviewer: ____________________

## Stage 2 — Fact check
- [ ] Every meaningful claim is traceable to a logged source at the correct tier.
- [ ] Safety-floor claims are **Verified/Officially confirmed** to **Tier 1–2**.
- [ ] Every claim has a verification date; none are **Outdated**.
- [ ] No **Community pattern** (Tier 4 lead) is stated as fact.
- Reviewer: ____________________

## Stage 3 — Local review (authenticity)
- [ ] **Name-swap test:** swapping the location name makes most sentences *false*, not merely awkward.
- [ ] A lifelong resident would recognize the place.
- [ ] A newcomer learns something genuinely useful.
- [ ] Every recommendation has evidence behind it.
- [ ] A local veterinarian / the parks department would read it without wincing.
- Reviewer: ____________________

## Stage 4 — Writing review
- [ ] Voice matches the manual (specific, calm, confident, never sensational, never padded).
- [ ] No swappable/generic sentences remain.
- [ ] Structure leads with what is most useful; context explains the "why."
- [ ] Ends with the "ask Lucy" and "save to My Pets" conversion edges.
- Reviewer: ____________________

## Stage 5 — SEO review (secondary outcome)
- [ ] Clean structure, honest metadata, appropriate schema.
- [ ] Canonical is correct; no self-competing duplicate.
- [ ] Zero reader-hostile optimization (nothing added for the algorithm alone).

## Stage 6 — Accessibility review

## Stage 8 — AI review (if AI drafted or assisted)
